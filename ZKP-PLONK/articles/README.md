### 📖 About This Project
This series is based on the framework and structure of the original work, with further **deconstruction and expansion**.

As someone with an **entirely liberal arts background**, I started learning PlonK from scratch. Grappling with mathematical formulas and protocol logic was tough, but much like PlonK itself, it gets better the more you explore it—like fine wine. Once you grasp the "patterns" in PlonK, your understanding of many other protocols deepens significantly.

With permission from the original author, this project reconstructs and organizes the content from a **complete beginner's perspective**.
